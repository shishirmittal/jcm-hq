Who we are

J.C. Mittal and Sons, Ratlam, trading as JCM Retails, is a wholesale and retail distributor of electrical, lighting, climate, automation and home appliance products. This policy explains how we handle information when we communicate with our customers and staff through WhatsApp and our business systems.

What we collect

Business contact details of our customers and suppliers (name, shop name, mobile number, city), our own sales, billing, payment and ledger records with them, and the messages you send to or receive from our WhatsApp number (including buttons you tap). For our staff we hold name, work mobile number and the tasks assigned to them.

How we use it

To send invoices, statements, payment reminders and order updates you have asked for or that relate to your account with us; to answer your questions; to take payment through UPI; and to run our business accounts. We do not use your information for advertising by others.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. Messages are delivered through WhatsApp (Meta Platforms) and stored on our own business systems and cloud database providers that host them for us. We share information with authorities only where the law requires.

How long we keep it

Business and tax records are kept for as long as the law requires. Chat messages are kept for as long as needed to serve your account.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to stop sending you messages, to correct your details, or to delete information we are not legally required to keep. Message us on WhatsApp at +91 91091 05999 or visit our shop at the address above and we will act on your request.

Security

Access to our systems is limited to our staff with sign-in, and access keys are stored only on our secure servers.

Changes

If we change this policy, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
